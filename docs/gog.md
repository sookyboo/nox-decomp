How to download `setup_nox_2.0.0.20.exe` from gog.com

Log in to your account on the gog website and buy the game if you haven't already

Click on the account drop down menu in the top right and click Games

In the search box search for "nox" 

Click the Nox game tile 

At the bottom expand "Download offline backup game installers"

Click on the row that says "Nox 525MB"
